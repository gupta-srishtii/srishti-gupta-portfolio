# Fine Art, Paintings & Digital Canvas
Place high-resolution visual artwork files here:
- Traditional paintings (scans/photos of oil, acrylic, watercolor works)
- Digital concept art and paintings
- Recommended format: `.webp` or `.avif`, max resolution 3840x2160, optimized under 800 KB
- Optional: `[painting-id]-depth.webp` grayscale depth maps for 3D parallax effects
